Practicalities

Good to know
before we meet.

A short list of the things people most often ask before a first conversation. If yours is not here, just ask.

Studio visits

We welcome visitors by appointment, Monday to Friday. Please email the studio at least two days ahead so we can make sure the right people are in.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within two working days, always. If your enquiry is urgent — a planning deadline, a site acquisition, a problem on site — say so in the subject line and we will prioritise it.

Do you charge for a first meeting?

No. The first conversation, whether in the studio or on site, is free and without obligation. We only charge once there is an agreed scope and a signed proposal.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Whatever you have. A site address, a survey, a mood board, a rough budget or simply a description of how you want to live or work. Drawings are helpful but never required — many of our best projects started with a conversation and a walk around the site.

Can we visit the studio?

Yes. We prefer to meet clients in person, surrounded by drawings and models. Email the studio to arrange a time, and let us know if you would like to see a particular project — we can usually arrange a visit to a completed building too.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Frequently. Roughly a third of our current projects are outside Switzerland and the UAE, and we run them with a mix of video reviews, shared models and regular site visits. For projects far from either studio we usually partner with a local architect of record.

How do I send drawings or large files?

Email us first and we will send a secure upload link. Please do not send large attachments unprompted — they often fail silently. For initial conversations, a few low-resolution images or a PDF under 10 MB is plenty.
