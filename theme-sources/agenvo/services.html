02 — Engineering

Structure as an idea, not an obstacle.

Our in-house structural and MEP engineers are part of the design conversation from the first meeting. That means fewer surprises, faster decisions, and structures that make the architecture possible rather than merely holding it up.

We specialise in long-span concrete, hybrid timber, and the careful intervention of existing structures. Every scheme is modelled, load-tested in principle, and costed before it leaves the studio.

  • Structural concept and detailed design
  • MEP coordination and building services
  • Facade engineering and thermal modelling
  • Existing-structure surveys and strengthening
  • Construction methodology and site supervision

03 — Interiors

Rooms that feel finished
before they are furnished.

Interior architecture is not decoration applied at the end. We design the shell, the light, the joinery and the furniture together, so that every element has a reason to be there and nothing fights for attention.

Spatial planning

We begin with how a room is used at 7am and at 11pm, then design the plan around those routines. Circulation, storage and sightlines are resolved before a single finish is chosen.

Bespoke joinery

Kitchens, libraries, wardrobes and staircases are drawn to the millimetre and made by workshops we have worked with for over a decade. Built-in, not bolted on.

Light and material

We layer daylight, architectural lighting and lamps so a room can shift from bright and social to quiet and intimate. Materials are chosen for how they feel barefoot, not just in photographs.

04 — Advisory & specialist services

Beyond the building.

Not every client needs a full architectural commission. Sometimes the most useful thing we can do is help you decide what to build, whether to buy a site, or how to make an existing building perform. These services can stand alone or sit alongside a design commission.

Feasibility & site appraisal

Before you commit capital, we test what a site can realistically hold: planning constraints, massing options, build cost, and the value of each scenario. You receive a clear recommendation, not a stack of drawings.

Typical duration — 3 to 6 weeks

Masterplanning & urban design

For districts, campuses and mixed-use sites, we establish the public realm, density, movement and phasing that allow individual buildings to be designed well later. We often work alongside landscape architects and transport consultants.

Typical duration — 3 to 9 months

Sustainability & carbon strategy

We set embodied and operational carbon budgets at concept stage, then track them through design. Services include lifecycle assessment, retrofit strategy, certification support (LEED, BREEAM, Minergie) and passive-design modelling.

Typical duration — ongoing

Adaptive reuse & heritage

The greenest building is usually the one already standing. We survey, assess and transform existing structures — offices, factories, villas — retaining what has value and inserting what a new use requires, with a light touch.

Typical duration — 6 to 18 months

Design review & second opinions

Already working with an architect? We offer independent design reviews for developers, boards and private clients — a candid assessment of the scheme, its risks and the highest-value changes before construction begins.

Typical duration — 1 to 3 weeks

Construction oversight

For clients building with another designer, we can act as the owner's representative on site: reviewing workmanship, chairing site meetings, managing variations and protecting the design intent through to handover.

Typical duration — project dependent

Fees & engagement

Clear from the start.

We do not publish a price list, because no two projects are the same. We do commit to a fixed scope and a transparent fee before work begins, and we never bill for time we did not agree.

Request a fee proposal

Percentage fee

For full architectural services, fees are typically a percentage of construction cost, staged against deliverables. This aligns our effort with the scale of the build and is standard practice across the profession.

Fixed fee

For well-defined scopes — a feasibility study, a single interior, a design review — we quote a fixed sum. You know the total before we start, and scope changes are agreed in writing.

Retainer

For developers and long-term clients, a monthly retainer provides ongoing design advice, site visits and planning support across multiple projects or a phased development.

What is included

Every proposal names the team, the deliverables, the number of review meetings and the exclusions. Travel, printing and specialist consultants are itemised separately so there are no surprises at invoice stage.

Common questions

Before you write.

A few things clients ask us most often. If your question is not here, ask it directly — we answer every enquiry personally.

Ask us anything
Do you work outside Switzerland and the UAE?

Yes. We have built in sixteen countries across Europe, the Middle East and Asia. For projects further afield we usually partner with a local architect of record while retaining design leadership, which keeps the process smooth across regulatory systems.

What size projects do you take on?

Our work ranges from a single room or staircase to masterplans of several hundred thousand square metres. What matters is not the size but whether the project offers a genuine design question and a client who wants to engage with it.

How early should we contact you?

As early as possible — ideally before you have purchased a site. A feasibility conversation can save significant cost and heartache later. That said, we regularly join projects at planning stage or even on site, and we are comfortable taking over an existing design.

Can you work with our existing architect or contractor?

Frequently. We provide design review, engineering, interiors or sustainability input to projects led by others, and we are happy to work alongside your contractor from the outset. Clear roles and a written scope keep these collaborations productive.

How do you approach sustainability?

As a design constraint, not an add-on. We set carbon budgets early, prioritise retention and low-carbon materials, model daylight and energy performance throughout, and report actual performance after occupancy. We are happy to work to LEED, BREEAM, Minergie or a bespoke standard.

Do you take on interior-only projects?

Yes. Interior architecture is a full service in its own right, led by Amara Okafor. We take on complete homes, hospitality interiors, and focused commissions such as kitchens, libraries or a single floor of a workplace.
