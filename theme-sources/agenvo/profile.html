Partners

The ones who sign
the drawings.

Agenvo is employee-owned and led by four partners. Each remains directly involved in projects — reviewing drawings, visiting sites, and sitting in the room when difficult decisions are made. There is no layer between the client and the people doing the work.

How the studio is organised

Four groups,
no silos.

Project teams are assembled around the needs of the brief, not the org chart. An architect, engineer and interior designer are assigned to every project from day one, supported by the specialist groups below.

Design studio

24 people

Architects and urban designers working across all sectors. Responsible for concept, planning and the drawings that define the building. Every project has a named design lead who stays with it from first sketch to handover.

Engineering

15 people

Structural, facade and building-services engineers. They sit in the same room as the architects and are involved from feasibility onward, so structural logic informs the design rather than arriving after it.

Interiors

13 people

Interior architects, furniture designers and a full-time material researcher. They work alongside the design team from concept stage and lead the studio's collaborations with craftspeople and manufacturers.

Delivery & research

12 people

Site architects, technical coordinators and a small research group working on low-carbon concrete, prefabricated stone and adaptive reuse. Research findings feed directly into live projects rather than sitting in a drawer.

Life at Agenvo

A studio that
makes room.

We work hard, but we do not mistake hours for output. The studio is set up to protect concentration, encourage argument, and let people have a life outside the building.

Protected Fridays

Friday afternoons are reserved for studio-wide reviews, research and model-making. No client meetings, no deadlines. It is where much of our best thinking happens.

Employee ownership

Since 2023 the studio has been majority employee-owned. Everyone who has been with us for three years receives a stake, and the partners are accountable to the whole studio.

Learning budget

Every person has an annual budget for courses, conferences and travel. We also fund one research sabbatical each year, open to anyone in the studio regardless of seniority.

Parental support

Six months of fully paid parental leave for all parents, with a phased return and no expectation of evening calls. Several of our project leads work four-day weeks.

Studio exchange

Every year, four people swap between Zurich and Dubai for a month. It keeps the two studios genuinely one practice, and gives younger staff international site experience.

Make things

The workshop is open to everyone, not just model-makers. Furniture prototypes, material tests and the occasional wonky chair come out of it. We believe designing is inseparable from making.
