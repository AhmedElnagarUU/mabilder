Who we are

An independent studio, deliberately small.

Model-making workshop, Zurich — where every project begins in the hand before it reaches the screen.

Agenvo was founded on a simple conviction: that architecture should serve the life inside it long after the photographs have faded.

We started in 2008 with two architects, a borrowed drafting table and a commission for a family house above Lake Zurich. Eighteen years later, the studio has grown to sixty-four people across Zurich and Dubai — but the working method has not changed. Every project still begins with a long conversation, a site visit in bad weather, and a physical model.

Our structure is intentionally integrated. Architects sit beside structural engineers, sustainability specialists and interior designers from the first sketch. This means fewer handoffs, fewer compromises, and a building that behaves as one coherent idea rather than a negotiation between disciplines.

We work across residential, commercial, cultural and hospitality projects, at scales from a single room to a district masterplan. What unites the work is not a signature style but a set of standards: honest materials, generous light, calm proportions, and details that reward a second look.

We are equally comfortable restoring a century-old facade and engineering a forty-storey tower. The common thread is care — for the client's ambition, the craftspeople who execute it, and the city that will live with it.

What we believe

Four principles.
No exceptions.

These are not marketing values. They are the questions we ask in every design review, on every project, at every scale.

01

Restraint

The most powerful move is often the one we remove. We edit relentlessly so that what remains — a stair, a window, a wall — can be experienced fully. Restraint is not minimalism for its own sake; it is clarity earned through iteration.

02

Material honesty

Stone should look like stone. Timber should age like timber. We specify materials for how they will look in twenty years, not how they photograph on opening day, and we detail them so they can be repaired rather than replaced.

03

Longevity

A building is a promise to a place. We design for adaptability, low operational carbon and graceful ageing, testing each scheme against a fifty-year horizon. The greenest building is the one that does not need replacing.

04

Collaboration

We are not authors working alone. Clients, engineers, craftspeople and future occupants all hold knowledge the building needs. Our job is to convene that knowledge early and keep it in the room until the last detail is resolved.

Our history

Eighteen years,
one conversation.

A studio evolves the way a building does — slowly, in layers, with each decision making the next one clearer. These are the moments that shaped how we work.

Meet the people
2008

Two architects, one table

Elena Marchetti and Jonas Frei establish Agenvo in a former print workshop in Zurich's Kreis 5. The first commission is a private house in Küsnacht, completed on a modest budget and still unaltered today.

2011

First international project

A lakeside pavilion on Lake Como brings the studio into Italy. The project establishes our long-running collaboration with local stonemasons and timber fabricators.

2014

Engineering brought in-house

Rather than outsourcing structure, we build a dedicated engineering team. The move halves coordination time on complex projects and gives architects direct access to structural thinking from day one.

2016

Dubai studio opens

A second studio opens in Dubai's Design District to serve clients across the Gulf, with a focus on commercial towers, hospitality and large-scale masterplanning.

2019

Water Pavilion wins the ARIA Prize

The pavilion is recognised for its cantilevered stone roof and zero-discharge water system. It remains our most-visited built work and a testing ground for low-impact construction.

2022

Carbon-neutral operations

Both studios transition to verified carbon-neutral operations. We publish our first project carbon assessments and begin specifying embodied-carbon budgets on every new commission.

2024

Corporate Tower completes

Our largest project to date opens in Dubai: 48 storeys, a publicly accessible sky lobby, and a facade that cuts solar gain by 38% against the local baseline.

2026

Sixty-four people, sixteen countries

The studio is now employee-owned, with a mandate to remain independent. Current research explores low-carbon concrete, prefabricated stone and the adaptive reuse of post-war office stock.

Inside the studio

Where the work happens

No corner offices, no closed doors. Models are built in the open, drawings are pinned to walls, and the best ideas usually arrive at the long table.

The open studio, Zurich — models, samples and drawings share the same table.

The people who hold the line

Elena Marchetti

Founding Partner, Architecture

Trained in Milan and Zurich, Elena leads design across the studio's cultural and residential work. She is a visiting critic at ETH and a stubborn defender of the physical model.

Jonas Frei

Founding Partner, Engineering

Jonas built the studio's engineering practice around a simple question: what is the least structure this idea needs? He leads long-span, timber and adaptive-reuse projects.

Amara Okafor

Partner, Interiors

Amara leads interior architecture and the studio's furniture collaborations. Her work is guided by tactility, daylight and the belief that rooms should be generous before they are beautiful.
